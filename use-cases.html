Scenario Library

Priority use cases for immediate value

Each case follows the same model: business problem, data challenge, Pollen approach, and expected outcomes.

Mobility and Transport Optimization

Business problem: Planning and execution teams cannot react quickly to disruptions.

Data challenge: Operational data is spread across scheduling, fleet, and customer systems.

Pollen approach: Build a shared operational model with trusted events, orchestrated flows, and real-time signals.

Expected outcomes: Lower delays, better utilization, and faster routing decisions.

Operations Efficiency

Business problem: Teams lose time reconciling reports before acting.

Data challenge: Definitions are inconsistent across departments and tools.

Pollen approach: Standardize core entities, enforce ownership, and automate cross-system reconciliation.

Expected outcomes: Faster cycle times, fewer manual tasks, and more predictable execution.

Revenue Generation from Data

Business problem: Data investment is treated as overhead instead of growth leverage.

Data challenge: No product model for exposing data capabilities internally or externally.

Pollen approach: Define data products, APIs, and contracts tied to commercial priorities.

Expected outcomes: New service opportunities, stronger customer value, and monetization readiness.
